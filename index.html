What happens each visit

Click a box to check it, or click and drag to check several at once. Click a column name to check or clear it for every visit. Undo reverses your last change. Use ↓ to copy a visit to all later visits. Starting layout: visit 1 office visit and x-ray, visit 2 adjustment, visit 3 adjustment and initial traction, then adjustment and traction. The 3 times a week, 4 weeks plan skips traction and initial traction. Re-exam visits (12th, 24th, 36th) are filled in for each plan. Picking a care plan resets the grid to that plan’s standard layout.
